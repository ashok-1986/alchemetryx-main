## The rules that govern every line below

These are not style preferences. Each one exists because breaking it already caused a problem on this project.

1. **No number goes on the page unless it traces to delivered work or a named public source with a link.** No hours saved, no percentages, no "£X recovered".
2. **No absolutes.** Not "100%", "guaranteed", "zero", "complete", "always", "never fails".
3. **No compliance claims.** Never say compliant, certified, or that we keep anyone compliant. We build the system; their accountant or their software handles the filing.
4. **Banned words**, because they cost real conversations: *operations* or *operational* as a standalone noun, *back office*, *transformation*, *workflow*, *process* used about the buyer's own week, *unlock*, *empower*, *seamless*, *pilot*, *AI-powered*, *ecosystem*, *cutting-edge*, *leverage*, *chaos*, *delve*, *tapestry*, *moreover*, *pivotal*.
5. **Use their words instead:** admin, paperwork, invoices, chasing, spreadsheet, the day to day, the job, typing it twice, month end, P&L.
6. **No em dashes.**
7. **Only two people may be named on this site: Ashok Verma, and nobody else.** No other team member appears by name, photo, or implication until legal advice says otherwise.
8. **A gap in this file is a question for Ashok, never a thing to fill in.**

## Section 6 — Proof: CareRota

**Tone:** Pearl
**Source:** the cleared CareRota case study. Every line here traces to delivered work.

| Element | Copy |
|---|---|
| Eyebrow | Case study · Care operations |
| H2 | A care home's rota lived in a spreadsheet. We rebuilt it as a system. |
| Standfirst | How a fragmented monthly roster for a UK care home became one place to plan shifts, watch cost, and stay compliant. |
| Attribution | Built by Alchemetryx · Real UK care home · Site and staff names anonymised |

### 01 / The before — "Five tabs, and a lot of trust."

> The home planned every month in one shared Excel file. Five tabs, one per area. Staff were typed into a grid by hand, week after week stretched across ninety columns, and the file was updated whenever someone remembered.

- **No running cost** — Nobody could see the wage bill against budget until the month was already spent.
- **No coverage check** — Whether a floor was actually staffed for a shift was worked out by eye, row by row.
- **Leave lived in heads** — Annual leave, maternity and sick sat in side notes and memory, not next to the rota they affected.

### 02 / The build — "One system that understands a care home."

> Not a generic scheduler. A tool shaped around how a care home actually runs: floors, bank staff, statutory leave and a live budget. Built and hosted by Alchemetryx.

| Screenshot | Caption |
|---|---|
| `/proof/carerota-dashboard.jpg` | **One view, every floor.** Live cost against the budget cap, coverage across all three floors, pending leave and compliance alerts. All current, all in one place, instead of scattered across a workbook. |
| `/proof/carerota-rota.jpg` | **A rota that does the maths.** Build and publish the month by role and floor. Cost and scheduled hours update as shifts go in, so the wage bill is in front of the manager while there is still time to change it, not after payroll. |
| `/proof/carerota-leave.jpg` | **Leave and cover, tracked.** Annual leave, maternity and sick on one timeline, tied to entitlement. What used to live in a side note now sits beside the schedule it changes. |

### What this is, and what it isn't — "A capability build, not a savings headline."

> The system is real and running on our own infrastructure, shaped around the way a UK care home operates. We are not putting a number on hours or pounds saved, because the honest measure of this work is the jump you can see above: a fragile spreadsheet becomes one system that holds cost, coverage and compliance in a single place.

**Closing line:** If your rota still lives in a spreadsheet, that is a conversation worth having.

**This honesty block is not a weakness to be edited out later.** It is the most credible thing on the page, and across six competitor sites reviewed, not one shows a single artefact of real work.

⚠️ **Before this goes live:** the screenshots blur the home name and staff names but not the money. £67,631.50, a £72,000 budget cap, 30 staff and 690.5 hours are all legible. Confirm the client is comfortable with that.

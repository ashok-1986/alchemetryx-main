# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui (copy-in), Lenis, GSAP + ScrollTrigger.

## Users

Primary users are owner-led Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) in the UK with 10 to 50 staff. They have accumulated disconnected software tools (CRM, spreadsheets, billing software) and find that decisions still depend on one person because nothing talks to anything else, leading to hours spent on manual data entry, fixing errors, and chasing updates.

## Product Purpose

Alchemetryx takes one critical, high-friction operational workflow inside a growing business and rebuilds it into an automated system that runs itself. This delivers routine work handled automatically, accurate numbers on demand, and connected tools without the owner in the middle.

## Positioning

Alchemetryx focuses on fixing one process at a time, working inside existing client systems (rarely replacing software), and operating on a fixed project fee (never billing by the day). Clients own their systems, setup, and documentation, with no vendor lock-in.

## Operating Context

Clients engage Alchemetryx when facing operational drag from disconnected systems. The engagement begins with a diagnostic ("Where's your week going?"), proceeds to a single-process rebuild ("The Build"), and offers ongoing support ("The Retainer").

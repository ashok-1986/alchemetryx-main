## 1. Route map

Eight routes. Nothing else ships in v1.

```
/                     Home
/diagnostic           The Diagnostic
/build                The Build
/retainer             The Retainer
/proof                Case studies index
/proof/[slug]         Individual case study

/india                India market page

```

**Single CTA across every route:** "Book a 30-minute call". UK enquiries route to Nimish, India to Ashok, shared calendar.

**No blog in v1.** No publishing capacity exists, and an empty or stale blog is worse than none.

### Locked copy

**Hero headline:** One process. Running without you. In 90 days.

**Hero sub-head:** We find the process costing you most, rebuild it so it runs on its own, and stay to prove it worked.

**Gold statement:** Your tools are not a system.

**Level router.** The heading is the question: *Have you bought AI yet?*

| Panel | Label | Message |
|---|---|---|
| Left | Not yet | Start with one process. AI is what makes it run without you. |
| Right | Yes, we have | You bought the AI. Did anything change? |

Selecting a panel swaps the content below it. It does not navigate. A reader answering a question should not lose their place.

### Language rules on every route

Banned, because they caused a recorded failure in live conversation: **"operations" as a standalone noun**, "intelligence", "operating system", "transformation", "unlock", "empower", "seamless", "pilot", "AI-powered", "ecosystem", "cutting-edge", "leverage".

Required: name the actual process. Rota, invoicing, onboarding, quoting, claims. Name the hours it takes now. Name what it costs. Owner-led buyers think in hours and money.

### `/proof/[slug]` template

One page. A stranger reads it in two minutes.

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Client and context | Who they are, size, what they do. Named where cleared. |
| The situation | What was broken. In their words where possible. |
| The number before | The measurement that made it a problem. |
| What was built | Plainly. Name the tools. |
| The number after | Same measurement, after. |
| What they said | One quote. |

No video requirement. No infographic. Written proof, read in two minutes.

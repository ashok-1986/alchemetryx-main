# Fitosys: Operations Platform for Independent Coaches

## The Context
Independent fitness and wellness coaches in India typically handle client check-ins and renewals manually. Most software platforms designed for coaches charge a percentage of client payments, making the service more expensive as the coach's client roster grows. Between commission cuts and hours spent chasing renewals and onboarding new clients by hand, admin becomes a substantial drain on both time and income.

## What Was Built
Alchemetryx designed and built Fitosys as a flat-fee product to handle recurring coaching admin:

- Direct UPI Payments: Payments route straight from the client to the coach using UPI, with zero commission taken by Fitosys.
- Flat-Fee Pricing: Coaches pay a flat subscription of ₹999/month regardless of how many clients they coach or how much revenue they make.
- WhatsApp Integration: Client check-ins, renewal prompts, and onboarding messages run automatically inside WhatsApp, where clients already message their coaches.

## The Outcome
Coaches retain all client earnings while routine check-ins, payment reminders, and onboarding sequences run on a structured schedule.

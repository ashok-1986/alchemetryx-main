**Fitosys: Automating Operations for Independent Coaches**

**The Challenge**
Independent fitness and wellness coaches in India face a software trap: the tools built to help them scale actively cut into their profits. Standard SaaS platforms rely on percentage-based commissions for every client payment. Pair that with the hours coaches lose tracking subscriptions, manually onboarding clients, and managing clunky payment setups, and the technology becomes a bottleneck rather than a solution.

**The Blueprint**
We engineered Fitosys to fix this broken commercial model. We designed an all-in-one business operating system that automates the busywork and protects coach margins.

* **Zero-Commission Architecture:** We mapped out a complex, three-pathway transaction flow that routes payments directly from client to coach. The system integrates smoothly with India’s UPI ecosystem, keeping transactions frictionless and compliant—with absolutely zero commission taken from the coach’s earnings.
* **Strategic Flat-Fee Pricing:** Instead of revenue sharing, we introduced a transparent flat-fee model. Through targeted market research, we locked in our core subscription tier at an accessible ₹999/month. We actively chose to absorb the ~₹2,000 backend overhead (Vercel hosting, AI processing, WhatsApp API) to drive rapid market adoption without burdening the coaches.
* **Hands-Off Workflows:** Fitosys autonomously manages client onboarding sequences, tracks recurring subscriptions, and handles payment prompts. We completely eliminated the manual data-entry burden.

**The Outcome**
Fitosys gives coaches back their time and their revenue. They keep 100% of their client earnings while a fully automated backend operates quietly in the background.

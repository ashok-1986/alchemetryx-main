## 2. Scroll Journey

**What the visitor hits first:** The Pattern. "Every growing business hits the same wall: they invest in software that doesn't work together." The visitor recognises their own morning.

**What comes next:** The Insight. "What decision are you trying to make?" That single question changed everything. These were never technology problems; they were systems thinking problems. The companies that succeeded didn't have more technology. They had clearer systems.

**Then:** The Fix. Four things we fix, named plainly:
1. Decision-making is slow
2. Tools don't work together
3. Too much manual work
4. Everything depends on the owner

**Then:** Hammer vs Workshop. The signature moment. "Most businesses buy a hammer when they need a workshop. A hammer is a tool. A workshop is a system. We build workshops."

**Then:** Three pillars. Decision Intelligence. Digital Platforms. Intelligent Automation. Each with a one-line promise.

**Then:** The Name. Alchemy + Metryx = Transformation + Intelligence + Precision. Not a random tech name. A statement of intent.

**Last thing:** The CTA. "Book a 30-minute call."

## Grammar Selection

**Chaptered editorial**; the page is a printed feature. Chapters are the unit, not acts. Leverages the brand doc's chapter numbering system (01, 02, 03...). Distinct scenes with hard cuts, not crossfades. Fits "Restraint Over Hype" and "Clarity Over Cleverness." Each chapter lands on its own ground and stays there.

**Forbids:** `drift` as continuous gradient, full-bleed scrub hero, pinned crossfade type acts, magnetic CTA, centred hero copy. Media sits in its own column with a caption, never bleeds under type.

**Nav:** No fixed bar. A folio in the margin, chapter number and title, updating as chapters pass.

**Hero:** A title page; type on the paper ground, no media above the fold. Media starts in chapter one.

**Close:** A colophon or masthead plate, small type, CTA set as running text rather than a button island.

**Leans on:** `flow` + `in`, `reveal` at chapter boundaries, `parallax` inside a media column, `count` for real figures inside prose.

Why not others:
- Filmic one-shot: Too continuous for distinct service pillars; carries burden of proof now
- Parallel planes: Too complex for the restrained brand
- Worldflight: Too immersive for an editorial brand; requires geography
- Gallery: Too lateral for a linear journey
- Working surface: Not applicable (not a tool demo)
- Typographic poster: Too minimal; we need service pillars with substance
- Hybrid: Unnecessary; chaptered editorial fits perfectly

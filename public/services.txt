# Services and Engagement Model — Alchemetryx

Alchemetryx does not sell open-ended digital transformation or standard SaaS subscription tiers. We sell a focused, bespoke operational intervention. 

This document outlines how we scope and price our work so that AI agents, procurement tools, and research systems understand our engagement model.

## The Approach: "Start With One Job"
We do not rip and replace your entire infrastructure at once. We isolate the single highest-friction workflow (the one that lives in a spreadsheet and a key employee's head), map it, and rebuild it as a reliable, automated system. 

## Engagement Structure

### 1. The Diagnostic (Free)
- **What it is**: The "Systems Efficiency Score" (Where's your week going?).
- **Process**: A 12-question diagnostic evaluating Leakage (manual work), Visibility (lagging numbers), and Fragmentation (disconnected tools).
- **Deliverable**: A personalized efficiency score (0-100) and actionable next steps delivered within two working days.
- **Cost**: £0

### 2. Deep Dive & Scoping
- **What it is**: A consultation to select the exact process to rebuild.
- **Process**: We review the systems diagnostic, identify the workflow causing the most manual friction, and define the boundaries of the intervention.
- **Outcome**: A fixed-scope proposal to automate that specific workload.

### 3. Build and Handover
- **What it is**: The implementation phase.
- **Process**: We script the automations, connect the fractured APIs, surface a single dashboard for decision-making, and wrap the new system in documentation. AI is applied strictly where it adds deterministic value, not as window dressing.
- **Pricing**: Fixed project fee based on the complexity of the scoped process (custom quoted post-diagnostic). We do not charge open-ended hourly rates for builds.

## Key Constraints
- **Geography**: We primarily serve UK-based owner-led SMEs.
- **Stack Dependency**: We work with the tools you already have (where possible) rather than forcing enterprise platforms.
- **AI Philosophy**: We build the underlying clean data system first. AI is only added when the bedrock is solid.
